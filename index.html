Tommy John surgery has become one of the most discussed procedures in professional baseball. What was once considered a career-threatening injury is now an increasingly familiar part of the sport.

The Rise of Tommy John

Using data collected by MLBPlayerAnalys, this graph examines the number of recorded Tommy John surgeries each year between 2000 and 2025.

Figure 1. Annual recorded Tommy John surgeries from 2000 to 2025. Source: MLBPlayerAnalys Tommy John Surgery List.

The data shows a substantial increase in recorded Tommy John surgeries over the past 25 years. In 2000, there were 29 recorded surgeries, compared with 176 in 2021, the highest year in the dataset. Although the number of surgeries fluctuates from year to year, the overall trend suggests that Tommy John surgery has become increasingly common in professional baseball.

What Does This Mean for Baseball?

Ever since the crave for higher velocity fastballs across all baseball leagues, the rate of injury has exponentially increased in all pitchers, especially major and minor leaguers. With the increase in velocity, comes an increase in risk of injury.
